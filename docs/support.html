Frequently Asked Questions

What is ClaudeScope?

ClaudeScope is a companion app for Claude AI users. It shows your real-time usage across 5-hour and 7-day rate limit windows, helping you plan your work and avoid unexpected limits.

Do I need a Claude account?

ClaudeScope works with any Claude Pro or Team subscription. You sign in using your Claude account via secure OAuth. The app also provides a demo preview without signing in.

What data does ClaudeScope access?

ClaudeScope only reads your usage statistics (utilization percentages and reset times) from the Anthropic API. It does not access your conversations, prompts, or any other personal data.

Is my data stored on a server?

No. All data is stored locally on your device. ClaudeScope does not operate any servers. Your credentials and usage history never leave your device.

How do I sign out or delete my data?

Go to Settings → Sign Out. This removes all stored credentials. You can also delete the app entirely, which removes all local data.

The app shows demo data. How do I see my real usage?

Tap "Sign in with Claude" on the home screen and complete the OAuth flow. Once signed in, your real usage data will replace the demo data automatically.

Which platforms are supported?

ClaudeScope is available for iOS and macOS. The iOS version requires iOS 18.0 or later. The macOS version requires macOS 14.0 or later.
