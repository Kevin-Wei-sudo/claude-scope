ClaudeScope Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 9, 2026

Overview

ClaudeScope is a utility app that helps you monitor your Claude AI usage. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

Data We Collect

Authentication Credentials: When you sign in, your OAuth access token and refresh token are stored locally on your device. These credentials are never transmitted to any server other than Anthropic's official API endpoints.

Usage Data: Your Claude usage statistics (utilization percentages and reset times) are fetched directly from Anthropic's API and stored locally on your device for history tracking.

Analytics: We use AppsFlyer and Facebook SDK for anonymous app analytics (e.g., feature usage, session data). No personal usage data from your Claude account is shared with these services.

Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your Claude conversations or prompts
  • We do not collect your email address or personal information
  • We do not sell any data to third parties
  • We do not store any data on our servers — all data stays on your device

Third-Party Services

Anthropic API: Used to fetch your Claude usage data. Subject to Anthropic's Privacy Policy.

AppsFlyer: Used for anonymous install attribution and app analytics. Subject to AppsFlyer's Privacy Policy.

Facebook SDK: Used for anonymous app analytics. Subject to Meta's Privacy Policy.

Data Storage

All user data (credentials, usage history, preferences) is stored locally on your device using the app's sandboxed storage. No user data is transmitted to or stored on any server operated by us.

Data Deletion

You can delete all stored data at any time by signing out within the app or by deleting the app from your device.

Children's Privacy

ClaudeScope is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated date.
